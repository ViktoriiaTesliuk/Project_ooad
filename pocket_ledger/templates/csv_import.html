Which files work?

A CSV with the header date,kind,amount,currency,category,description: dates as YYYY-MM-DD, expense or income, positive amounts, the wallet’s currency, and an existing category name. Description is optional.

Monobank statements also work directly. Choose a wallet in the card’s currency; food and transport purchases are categorized from their MCC codes and everything else uses Other.
